# Research base 2016–2026 and validation results

This document separates **evidence** (published, peer-reviewed or reproducible results), **hypotheses** (plausible ideas, not proven) and **our own measurements** (reproducible with `npm run fetch-binance` and `npm run research`).

## 1. Literature: what is reasonably established

| Topic | Finding | Source | Strength | How it is used in the bot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Time-series momentum | Crypto returns show a strong time-series momentum effect. Crypto has little exposure to common stock-market and macro factors. | Liu & Tsyvinski, *Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency*, Review of Financial Studies 2021 ([NBER w24877](https://nber.org/papers/w24877)) | Strong (peer-reviewed), measured at daily/weekly horizons | `trend` strategy (EMA alignment + positive 7-day return) |
| Technical rules | Technical rules show predictability that survives data-snooping corrections. Break-even costs are high, **but BTC showed no predictability out of sample**. | Hudson & Urquhart, *Technical trading and cryptocurrencies*, Annals of Operations Research 2019 ([link](https://hull-repository.worktribe.com/output/2328042)) | Moderate | `trend`/`breakout` are tested strictly out of sample, never assumed |
| Short-horizon reversal | About 90% of crypto pairs show a reversal at 15 min (it fades by 1h and is gone at 4h). The gross edge is about 1.3 bp, well under spot costs (5 bp maker, 10–20 bp taker), so it is **not exploitable** at spot costs. | Kitron & Wengrowicz, arXiv 2608.21888, Aug 2026 ([link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.21888)). The authors run a trading system based on it. | Moderate (recent preprint) | Explains why the 1-minute "early momentum" bursts faded in our replays. Minute scalping is rejected. |
| Fake volume | Wash trading averaged more than 70% of reported volume on unregulated exchanges. | Cong, Li, Tang & Yang, *Crypto Wash Trading*, NBER w30783 (2022) ([link](https://nber.org/papers/w30783)) | Strong | Volume filters use the most liquid regulated reference market. Volume alone never triggers a trade. |
| Backtest overfitting | Selecting the best of many backtests inflates the Sharpe ratio, and the result must be deflated for the number of trials. | Bailey & López de Prado, *The Deflated Sharpe Ratio* ([SSRN 2460551](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551)), *The Probability of Backtest Overfitting* ([SSRN 2326253](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253)) | Strong (methodology) | Walk-forward with frozen parameters, a small grid, robustness and cost-sensitivity gates |
| Volatility management | Scaling exposure inversely to volatility improves risk-adjusted returns in traditional assets. Evidence specific to crypto is mostly practitioner material. | Moreira & Muir (traditional assets); [Research Affiliates note](https://www.researchaffiliates.com/publications/press-exclusive/1014-harnessing-volatility-targeting/jcr:content/theme/main/publication/body/publication_parsys_484942854) | Moderate (crypto: hypothesis) | Position size is scaled down when daily volatility is above 4% (risk control, not an alpha claim) |
| Stablecoin risk | USDC lost its $1 peg for a weekend in March 2023 after the Silicon Valley Bank failure, then re-pegged. | e.g. [Yahoo/Decrypt report](https://autos.yahoo.com/usdc-stablecoin-breaks-dollar-peg-following-silicon-valley-bank-collapse-232052571.html) | Factual event | The quote currency is not risk-free. A future improvement is a USDC/USD depeg monitor (not implemented). |

**Not used, or treated as hypotheses only:** halving cycles (too few observations: 4 halvings since 2012), "altseason" rotation timing, support and resistance (not reproducibly defined), RSI divergences (no robust evidence after costs), and machine learning (about 80k hourly bars per asset with strong regime changes is far too little to fit ML models without overfitting). Kelly sizing is not used: the edge estimates are too uncertain, and full or fractional Kelly on a noisy estimate maximises ruin risk. Fixed-fraction risk sizing (1% per trade) is used instead.

## 2. Data actually used
- **Prices:** Binance spot `BASEUSDT` 1-hour candles from 2017-08 to 2026-10, via the public API (`npm run fetch-binance`). Revolut X only serves candles since its own launch, so it cannot cover 2018–2023.
- **Execution costs:** Revolut X taker fee of 0.09% per side, plus the **Revolut X USDC spread snapshot** of each coin, plus 0.05% slippage per side.
- **Universe:** coins tradable against USDC on Revolut X. Coins whose current Revolut X spread exceeds 0.4% are excluded, because the live bot refuses them too. 8 coins remained (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, LINK, TRX, BONK). The 15 others were excluded on spread.
- **Biases that remain:** *survivorship bias*, since the coin list is today's Revolut X list. *Spread bias*, since today's spread is applied to the whole history. Binance prices are used as a proxy for Revolut X prices. Partial fills are not simulated on hourly bars, and orders are assumed to fill at the next open plus costs.

## 3. Method
- Decisions are made at the close of a 1h bar, using only data up to that bar, and fill at the **next open** (tested).
- **Walk-forward:** 12 months of training, then 3 months of test, rolling every 3 months (31 windows, Dec 2018 – Sep 2026). Parameters are chosen on the training window only (4 sets per strategy, by Sharpe ratio with at least 8 trades). A strategy is enabled in a test window only if its training Sharpe is above 0 and its profit factor above 1.
- **Same engine as live:** the decision engine, risk engine (1% risk per trade, 5/3/2 slots, 3% monthly loss limit with flatten, 10% drawdown halt, correlation guard, loss-streak pause) and costs are identical.
- **Baselines:** cash, BTC buy-and-hold, and equal-weight buy-and-hold.
- **Robustness gates:** at least 75% of parameter sets must be profitable over the full period, the strategy must stay profitable at a 0.2% spread, and the out-of-sample t-statistic must be ≥ 2.

## 5. Conclusions
1. **No strategy meets the robustness bar for real money.** `trend` has a statistically positive out-of-sample result (t = 2.26). But its sign flips across neighbouring parameters, and it only works with spreads at or below 0.2% (BTC, ETH, SOL on Revolut X). The live bot therefore **does not trade** in quant mode unless `strategy-validation.json` validates a strategy.
2. **The 5%/month objective is not supported by the data.** The best robust-ish result is about +0.6%/month on average with a 27% drawdown. Even BTC buy-and-hold, with a far worse risk profile, averaged far below 5% in most months.
3. **With 10 USDC, costs dominate.** A round trip costs 0.37–0.48% (0.02–0.025 USDC on 5 USDC). Over 7.6 years the bot paid **more fees than its starting capital**. The 3% monthly limit (0.30 USDC) equals about 3 planned stop-losses at 1% risk, or about 13 round trips of pure costs.
4. **What would change the picture** (not proven, worth testing): maker (post-only) entries, since Revolut X maker fees are 0%; longer holding periods (daily bars); restricting to BTC/ETH/SOL, where spreads are tightest.
